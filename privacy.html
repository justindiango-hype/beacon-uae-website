Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy explains how Al Manal BEACON LLC (“BEACON”, “we”, “us”) handles personal data when you visit beaconuae.com or contact us about our business setup, visa, PRO, banking and tax advisory services.

1. Who we are

Al Manal BEACON LLC, Office 1006, Al Amal Street, Business Bay, Dubai, United Arab Emirates. For any privacy question or request, email info@beaconuae.com.

2. What this website collects

The website itself does not ask you to create an account, does not use analytics or advertising trackers, and does not set its own cookies. The tax calculator and the FAQ assistant run entirely in your browser: what you type into them is not sent to us or stored.

Some parts of the page are provided by third parties, which receive your IP address and basic browser information when the page loads:

  • Google Maps, for the embedded office map. Google may set cookies under its own privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts and public code libraries (cdnjs, jsDelivr, unpkg), which deliver the fonts and scripts that display the site.

3. Information you give us

When you contact us by WhatsApp, phone or email, or book a consultation, we receive the information you choose to share. This usually includes your name, phone number, email address, nationality, and details about your business plans. If you become a client, we also collect the documents needed for your company setup, visas, bank accounts or tax registration, such as passport copies, photographs, Emirates ID details, proof of address, shareholder and financial information.

These channels are run by third parties with their own privacy terms: WhatsApp (Meta), Zoho Bookings (Zoho) and your email provider. If you follow our Instagram or Facebook links, those platforms' terms apply.

4. How we use your information

  • To answer your questions and arrange consultations.
  • To prepare proposals and deliver the services you engage us for.
  • To submit applications to the authorities and institutions involved in your setup.
  • To meet our legal, regulatory and record-keeping obligations, including anti-money-laundering checks.
  • To keep you informed about your case. We will only send marketing messages if you agree to them, and you can opt out at any time.

We process your data because it is needed to take steps at your request or to perform our contract with you, to comply with the law, for our legitimate interest in running and improving our services, or with your consent where consent is required.

5. Who we share it with

We share personal data only where needed to deliver your services or meet legal duties. This includes free zone and mainland licensing authorities, immigration and residency authorities, medical and Emirates ID service centres, banks you apply to, and accountants, auditors or legal advisers working on your matter. We also use service providers such as our email, messaging and booking tools. We do not sell personal data.

6. International transfers

Many of our clients are based outside the UAE, and some of our service providers store data abroad. Where personal data leaves the UAE, we take reasonable steps to make sure it stays protected, in line with UAE law and, where it applies, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

7. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information for as long as we need it to respond and follow up. We keep client records for as long as the engagement lasts and afterwards for the period required by UAE commercial, tax and anti-money-laundering laws. After that, we delete or anonymise the data.

8. Keeping it secure

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. Only staff and partners who need it to do their work have access.

9. Your rights

Under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021) and, where it applies, the GDPR, you can ask us to:

  • Give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Delete your data, where we no longer have a legal reason to keep it.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing, including direct marketing.
  • Provide your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent you have given, at any time.

To make a request, email info@beaconuae.com. We may need to confirm your identity first. You also have the right to complain to the relevant data protection authority.

10. Children

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children through this website.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
