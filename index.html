Why the UAE?

The World's Most Tax-Efficient Business Jurisdiction

The UAE offers an unmatched combination of financial sovereignty, global connectivity, and zero personal taxation—making it the premier destination for international entrepreneurs.

0%

Corporate Tax

Applicable only on net profits exceeding €95k EUR annually—one of the world's most competitive tax regimes.

0%

Personal Income Tax

Keep 100% of what you earn personally. Zero taxation on salary, bonuses, and executive compensation—forever.

0%

Tax on Dividends & Royalties

Seamless corporate repatriation of wealth globally. Distribute profits and royalties to shareholders free of withholding tax.

0%

Standard VAT

A minimal 5% standard VAT rate on domestic consumption—the lowest among major global economic hubs.

0%

Total Asset Protection

Complete operational freedom to run your business globally from anywhere, with robust legal structures protecting your assets.

Execution Roadmap

From Strategy to Official Trade License

Five structured milestones to launch and scale your UAE business with total operational peace of mind.

STEP 01 · PLAN WITH CLARITY

Consultation & Strategy

We learn about your business model, goals, activities, visa requirements and expansion plans, then help identify the UAE setup route that fits your needs.

STEP 02 · GET THE PAPERWORK READY

Onboarding & Documentation

Our team guides you through document collection and verification, confirms the proposed company details, and coordinates trade name reservation and the required applications.

STEP 03 · MAKE IT OFFICIAL

Official Trade License

We coordinate the licensing process and help you understand the official company documents issued for your chosen jurisdiction and business activity.

STEP 04 · YOUR RESIDENCY JOURNEY

Visa & Residency Support

Where included and applicable, we help coordinate the residency visa process, medical fitness appointment and Emirates ID biometrics, and guide you through the next steps.

STEP 05 · PREPARE FOR WHAT'S NEXT

Banking & Business Growth

We help you understand corporate banking requirements and, where appropriate, facilitate introductions and support you in preparing for bank compliance checks. Account approval remains with the bank.

Step 1 of 5

Timelines, approvals and requirements vary by jurisdiction, activity and individual circumstances.
